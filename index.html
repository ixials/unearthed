WHAT IS
UNEARTHED?
I love maps and museums, so I wanted to try combining both into a kind of live museum that aggregates archaeological and paleontological discoveries in almost real space-time. This is a prototype of that idea, relying on a lot of free tier software subscriptions and fake homemade machine learning. As you will find, some of the results are pretty accurate, and others might be completely scuffed. Either way, every week we are finding enough things from thousands of years ago to populate this map! You know what they say about archaeologists — every discovery is groundbreaking! Hahaha.
These articles are pulled from a basic keyword search query and funneled through some very messy custom filters that try to prevent Mapbox from putting text about Athens, Greece into Athens, Georgia. There are problems that I will not go in detail about, but I've left the geocoding at about 95% accuracy. Eventually, I might make some kind of model that is really good at deducing where exactly some dinosaur was dug up from a noisy headline and excerpt. (Also, if the markers aren't showing up, it's probably because we've maxed out NewsAPI's daily retrieval limit.) For now, the limitations of this app are more or less the limitations of the software, but feel free to experiment with the code!
CODING
CREDITS
Unearthed is using NewsAPI for real-time news data with a time delay of 24 hours, spaCy for named entity recognition, and Mapbox for geocoding. The map is built with Leaflet and styled with tiles from Jawg, and date picking is enabled by flatpickr. We are location matching against datasets of cities, states, and countries pulled from the lovely Internet. Many thanks to everyone who coded these things for making this so much easier, and also shoutout Amanda Askell <3 All code can be found on GitHub.
